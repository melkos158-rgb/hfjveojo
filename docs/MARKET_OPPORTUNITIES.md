# Market opportunities

Evidence first. An opportunity counts as validated only when someone pays. Prices below come from public pages read on the date shown. "Hypothesis" means not yet tested with money.

## Scoring

Each opportunity is scored 1–5 on:

- **Evidence**: do people already pay for this?
- **Margin**: price vs delivery cost.
- **Acquisition**: how easily can we reach buyers without an ad budget?
- **Speed**: how fast to the first dollar?
