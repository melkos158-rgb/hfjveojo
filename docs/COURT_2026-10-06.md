# Court of agents on ORVIONIS (6 Oct 2026)

The owner asked to put ORVIONIS "before a court of agents" (question chosen by him: continue as is, change, or stop).
Run as a workflow (`wf_080e461d-081`, 9 agents, ≈ 10 minutes) on a case file of real numbers only (day 11: $0 revenue,
626 page views / 149 sessions in 30 days, EUR 29.98 ads + $4.08 AI, 10 DMs → 1 reply, 9 competitors' prices).

- Expert witnesses: A, market and competition; B, channels and unit economics (web research with sources).
- Counsels: prosecution (STOP), defence (CONTINUE), pivot counsel (CHANGE: real-estate photographers at $8 wholesale).
- Fact-checker: tested every side's key claims against the case file and the cited URLs.
- Judges: numbers, market and product, execution and the founder's time.

## Verdict: CHANGE, 3–0 (confidence 60, 60, 62)

Keep the product. Change how the owner's time is spent. Stopping now would throw away a test that hasn't run: the
one channel both witnesses rate as viable, messages the owner sends himself, has had 10 sends and 0 follow-ups.
Continuing as is was rejected too, because videos, group posts and Fiverr took owner minutes and returned 0 tagged visits.

Consensus steps:
1. **Owner sends** the 10 pending follow-ups and batch 2 by 9 Oct (20 touches), then at least 5–6 touches a day, about
   1 in 4 to US real-estate photographers who don't deliver through Aryeo. The operator prepares a daily copy-paste
   send pack (EN + UA) and a touch log. Target: 50+ touches by 20 Oct, 120+ by 5 Nov.
2. **Same-day free photo** link for every reply. The owner self-tests the free photo by 9 Oct; Stripe payouts enabled
   by 20 Oct. Target: 3+ real free-photo claims by 27 Oct, the first paid order by 5 Nov.
3. **Convert:** follow up every free-photo user within 48 h. Target: 3+ paid orders from 2+ customers by 24 Dec, the
   minimum bar for continuing after day 90 (gate 2, 10 orders or AOV ≥ $35, is a stretch goal).

Paused at once: owner minutes on TikTok, Shorts, Instagram posting, Facebook group posts and Fiverr (the operator keeps
shipping SEO at zero owner time). E8b (EUR 20): the numbers judge says no (negative expected value: ≈ 15 clicks,
estimated EUR 54–217 per paying customer); the other two allow it only after the free-photo self-test.

Stop rule (passive mode: site and checkout stay live, 0 owner minutes, 0 new spend), any one of:
- fewer than 50 owner-sent touches by 20 Oct (the experiment isn't being run);
- 100+ touches by 5 Nov with 0 free-photo claims and 0 paid orders;
- 5+ free-photo claims and 0 paid orders by 15–20 Nov (after one price or quality fix);
- fewer than 3 paid orders by 24 Dec → close-out at the 25 Dec review.

Minority refinement (market judge): make a per-listing bundle the main offer (for example 4 rooms, 2 versions each, $39,
≈ $9.75 a photo), lead with "whole listing in minutes, no subscription", and aim outreach at agents outside CRMLS
(Florida's Stellar MLS first). Prices are the owner's call.

Rejected: the $8 photographer-wholesale pivot as the main bet. Zillow gives Aryeo subscribers its staging "at no extra
cost", and the pivot's 15–30 owner-minutes per order counted one trial as one repeat customer.

  - **Listing Pack $49**: up to 5 rooms of one listing + the MLS description and social captions, preselected and quoted

  - **Finish this listing $39** on the free-photo result page and in its delivery email: 4 more rooms of the same

  - The "one price fix" from session 1: if 4+ real free-photo claims give 0 paid orders by 10 Nov, the Listing Pack

## How the operator implements it

- Price function for one listing: 1–3 rooms at $15 each; 4–5 rooms → the $49 pack; each room beyond 5 adds $10, so
  10 rooms = $99 (the ruled "10 rooms $99" without a $49 → $99 cliff at 6 rooms, which the rulings left unspecified).
- Previews only after the agent replies and agrees, from a photo he sends or approves. Two judges preferred staging
  rooms from public listings before any contact; the operator doesn't, because listing photos are copyrighted (often by
  the photographer) and MLS/IDX rules limit their use. The market judge's version (after a reply) is used.
- Houston prospects in the tracker are held; nothing is sent to Southern California CRMLS areas.
- Owner, 8 Oct: 11 messages sent (10 new from the daily pack + the Lesley reminder), so 20 touches in all, the "20 by 9 Oct"
  step done a day early; the free-photo self-test passed (order #8: email click 18:46 UTC, delivered the same minute).
- Stripe payouts: `/admin/system` reads the live account as "charges enabled · payouts enabled · details submitted"
  (seen 9 Oct 12:58 UTC; on 26 Sep payouts were not enabled), so the owner's "payouts by 20 Oct" step is done. Of the
  E8b conditions only "1 real paid order from 4 or fewer free-photo claims" is still missing.
- Lever 2 shipped on 8 Oct (s100): `/admin/orders/preview` stages one room of a prospect's listing from a photo they sent
  or approved, on the order page opened by its private token, with "Finish this listing" ($39, 7 days) and a direct link
  to it. The admin gets the link and the reply to paste. Previews are reported next to free-photo claims, not inside them
  (`/admin/analytics` shows both), so the 27 Oct gate keeps its original meaning; the check-ins report both numbers.
- Lever 3 shipped on 6 Oct (s91), ahead of the 20 Oct date: `/tools/pro-credits` ($149, 25 rooms, valid 12 months,
  unbranded output; the balance is computed from orders, used by signing in with the buying email) and `/photographers`

  from payment; the refund policy adds a full refund within 14 days when no room was used.
